Introduction

Defendant Nexus Innovations LLC ("Defendant") respectfully moves this Court to dismiss Plaintiff Techno Corp.'s ("Plaintiff") Complaint pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(b)(2) for lack of personal jurisdiction.

As set forth in the accompanying Memorandum of Points and Authorities, Defendant is not a resident of California, maintains no continuous or systematic contacts with this forum, and the claims alleged do not arise out of any forum-related activities.

Accordingly, exercising jurisdiction over Defendant would offend traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice. See Int'l Shoe Co. v. Washington, 326 U.S. 310, 316 (1945).
